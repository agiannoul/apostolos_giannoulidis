Research

My work follows the path from raw sensor data to a maintenance decision. Real cases from Philips factory, Framatome hydrogen fuel stations and connected vehicle fleets motivate each step.

  1. DetectUnsupervised learning on non-stationary streams
  2. ExplainContext- and causality-aware anomaly detection
  3. Predict whenExplainable time-to-event prediction

Step 1

Detect early signs in data that keeps changing

Machines run under changing settings and have few labelled failures. How can detection, thresholds and clustering stay reliable without labels or manual tuning?

Try it: why a fixed threshold breaks under drift

Synthetic data, for illustration.

Threshold type

The score shifts when the machine changes setting, not because it is failing. Moving 2T recomputes the threshold from recent data as mean + 2σ, removes the outliers, and repeats.

Step 2

Explain the alarm with its context

Many alarms reflect a change in operating context rather than a fault. How to separate the two, and explain detections through causal relations between context and sensor data?

Try it: prune alarms that the context explains

Synthetic data, for illustration.

Context links each alarm to what else happened at the time, such as a speed change or a holiday, found with causal discovery. An alarm caused by such an event is explained instead of raised.

TECAD: explainable contextualized anomaly detection

Journal of Intelligent Information Systems 2026IF 3.5

Builds the context of any anomaly detector from heterogeneous sources and causal discovery, for explanations and better decisions.

PaperCode

Feedback- and causality-enriched multimodal context

IEEE Access 2025IF 4.2

Fuses discrete and continuous streams with feedback on past false alarms, to prune similar false alarms before they are raised.

Step 3

Predict when an asset will fail, and what would delay it

Failure data are often censored: a machine is replaced before it breaks. How to compare Remaining Useful Life (RUL) regression with survival models, and find feasible counterfactuals?

Try it: from a point prediction to a survival curve, and back

The translation used by TITEUF, on toy values.

TITEUF: model selection for time-to-event tasks

KDD 2026CORE A*

A unified framework to select among RUL and survival models, compared with both families of metrics.

PaperCode

CESA: counterfactual explanations for survival analysis

EDBT 2027CORE B

Finds concise, feasible changes that would improve an individual's predicted survival, while keeping the shape of the survival curve.
